## About Me

I am a Ph.D. student at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou), advised by Prof. Ying-Cong Chen. My research focuses on AI agents and generative models, especially general-purpose generation systems, multimodal and embodied reasoning, affordance understanding, and controllable visual generation.

Before that, I received my Bachelor's degree in Mathematics-Physics Fundamental Science from Yingcai Honors College at the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC). During my undergraduate studies, I worked on visual generation research at the UESTC Center for Future Media with Prof. Lianli Gao and Prof. Jingkuan Song. I was also a visiting student/intern at Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence (MBZUAI), working with Prof. Qiang Sun.


## Research Interests

- **AI Agents and General-Purpose Generation**
- **Multimodal and Embodied Reasoning**
- **Affordance Understanding and Tool Use**
- **Controllable Visual Generation**
- **Latent Reasoning and Representation Learning**
